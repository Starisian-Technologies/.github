# Code of Conduct  
**Starisian Technologies**

Like the broader creative and technical communities we serve, the Starisian Technologies team is made up of a growing group of builders, artists, linguists, educators, and volunteers working across the globe. We collaborate on tools for cultural preservation, storytelling, education, music, and economic empowerment.

We come from diverse backgrounds — and that is one of our greatest strengths. But diversity can also bring challenges in communication, expectation, and culture. This Code of Conduct exists to help us build a safe, respectful, and open space where everyone can participate fully and with dignity.

This Code applies to all spaces managed by Starisian Technologies and its projects. That includes our GitHub repositories, discussion boards, virtual events, project-related forums, and any collaborative spaces we maintain. In some cases, violations outside these spaces may still affect someone’s ability to participate within them.

If you believe someone is violating this code, please report it to:  
📩 **conduct@starisian.com**

---

## 💡 Our Ground Rules

### Be friendly and patient.  
Assume good intent. We're all here to learn, build, and contribute together — sometimes at different speeds.

### Be welcoming.  
We strive to support people of all identities, abilities, and experiences — including those across races, cultures, genders, sexual orientations, class, religion, language, neurotype, or nationality. This is a global, inclusive community.

### Be considerate.  
Your decisions will affect others — their work, their time, and sometimes their reputation. Consider how your words or code affect the people around you, especially across language and cultural boundaries.

### Be respectful.  
We won’t always agree. That’s okay. What’s not okay is disrespect, exclusion, harassment, or harm. Disagreements must never become personal attacks. No project is more important than the dignity of the people contributing to it.

### Be careful with words.  
Kindness costs nothing. Do not insult, dismiss, or attack. The following behavior is never acceptable:
- Threats or acts of violence
- Hate speech, slurs, or discrimination of any kind
- Sexually explicit or graphically violent content
- Doxing or publishing someone’s private information
- Repeated harassment or unwanted contact
- Unwelcome sexual attention or commentary

If someone asks you to stop — stop.

### When we disagree, try to understand.  
Different people have different perspectives, experiences, and pain points. Try to ask clarifying questions. Focus on solutions, not blame. Give others space to explain. And when you’re wrong? Own it. We all mess up. What matters is how we grow.

## 🧭 Our Commitment

We, as contributors, maintainers, users, and community leaders of Starisian Technologies and its projects, pledge to foster an open, welcoming, diverse, and harassment-free environment.

We are committed to making participation in our projects a respectful and empowering experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or cultural identity.

---

## 🌟 Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- Demonstrate empathy, kindness, and patience.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, cultures, and experiences.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language — especially across languages and dialects.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Apologize and take responsibility when you make mistakes — and learn from them.
- Prioritize what is best for the community, not just individual needs.

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment of any kind (public or private)
- Sexually explicit or violent material
- Discriminatory language or personal attacks
- Posting (or threatening to post) others’ private information (“doxing”)
- Repeated or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, baiting, or disruptive behavior
- Use of our tools for surveillance, cultural extraction, or unethical profiling

If someone asks you to stop, **stop immediately**.

## 📍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All online and offline spaces managed by Starisian Technologies
- GitHub repositories, discussions, wikis, social media, and virtual events
- Any instance where a person is officially representing the project

Conduct violations outside these spaces **may** impact a person’s participation in the community, especially if they harm others involved.

---

## ⚙️ Enforcement Guidelines

We follow a community impact scale for determining consequences:

### 1. Correction
**Impact:** Minor, unintentional behavior  
**Consequence:** Private warning. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
**Impact:** Single or repeated violations  
**Consequence:** Formal warning with boundaries. Any further violation may result in temporary or permanent exclusion.

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Impact:** Serious violation or pattern of harm  
**Consequence:** Temporary removal from all community interaction.

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Impact:** Continued violations, harassment, or harm  
**Consequence:** Permanent removal from the community and all Starisian-affiliated projects.

---

## 🛠️ Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are empowered to:

- Clarify and uphold the Code of Conduct in all community spaces  
- Investigate reports with discretion and respect for all parties  
- Temporarily restrict participation or moderate content to de-escalate harm or maintain safety

These decisions are made **in collaboration with Starisian Technologies staff** and, where appropriate, other trusted members of the project community.

> While community leaders may act quickly to address urgent issues, they do not act alone or with final authority. Longer-term or complex enforcement decisions are reviewed collectively.

Our approach to enforcement promotes **collaboration and protection**, ensuring our community remains a safe, constructive, and inclusive environment for everyone involved.
